Work / Project 04

REDMS

A Real Estate & Developers Management System, built for a real client through meetings, requirement gathering and feedback cycles, through to final delivery.

Client
Real estate & development company
Stack
C# WinForms (.NET Framework), SQL Server 2019
Type
Semester project
Access
Separate dashboards for CEO, Managing Director, Social Media Manager

Overview

One platform for running several construction projects at once: materials, suppliers, labour, sales and progress.

REDMS was developed with a client in the real estate industry. The work went through iterative client meetings, requirement gathering and feedback cycles before final delivery, so every module maps to how the company actually runs its sites.

Features

01

Materials & suppliers

Tracks materials such as cement and steel along with their suppliers.

02

Labour payroll

Payroll management with role-based filters.

03

Unit sales

Monitors the sales status of every unit.

04

Phase updates

Project phases with progress percentages and image uploads.

05

Reports

Exportable financial reports, graphs and charts.

06

Secure roles

Login with separate dashboards for the CEO, Managing Director and Social Media Manager.

Scale

Built to manage multiple projects from a single platform.

Instead of one database per site, REDMS keeps every project in one place, so leadership can compare progress, spending and sales across developments.
