Work / Project 03

The Literacy Illusion

An analysis of Pakistan’s education data that looks past the headline figures to the disparities in literacy and enrollment underneath.

Context
Datafest, Pakistan Bureau of Statistics
Data
Pakistan Education Statistics (PES)
Stack
Power BI, Python
Outcome
Presented at Datafest; volunteer participation certificate

Overview

A single literacy rate hides a lot. Broken down by region, gender and stage of schooling, the same data tells a very different story.

Working with Pakistan Education Statistics (PES), I analysed the data in Python to surface hidden trends, then built an interactive Power BI report so the disparities in literacy and enrollment could be explored rather than just read.

Report pages

What I did

  1. Analysed PES data to surface trends that the national averages hide.
  2. Built an interactive Power BI report showing disparities in literacy and enrollment.
  3. Designed drill-down views that move from national KPIs to provinces and districts on a map.
  4. Presented the findings at Datafest (PBS) and received a volunteer participation certificate.
